Design and generate a complete modern web application UI for a project called "TTD Smart Pilgrimage Platform".

This platform is built for Tirupati Balaji Temple and uses AI-powered forecasting to predict future pilgrim arrivals and provide intelligent recommendations for pilgrims, hotels, travel agencies, and temple management.

IMPORTANT DESIGN REQUIREMENTS:

Do NOT create a dark cyber-security dashboard, terminal interface, fintech dashboard, or developer-style SaaS design.

The design should feel:

* Spiritual
* Trustworthy
* Elegant
* Premium
* Peaceful
* Modern
* Welcoming

The visual experience should reflect the cultural and spiritual significance of Tirupati Balaji Temple.

Color Palette:

* Temple Gold (#B8860B)
* Ivory White (#FFF8E7)
* Sandalwood Brown (#8B4513)
* Soft Cream backgrounds
* White cards with subtle shadows
* Minimal use of dark colors

Typography:

* Elegant modern typography
* Large headings
* Clear readability
* Premium travel and tourism website feel

Visual Elements:

* High-quality temple photography
* Pilgrims and temple surroundings
* Beautiful hero sections
* Smooth animations
* Glassmorphism where appropriate
* Rounded cards
* Professional illustrations
* Clean spacing and modern layout
